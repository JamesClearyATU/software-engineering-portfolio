## The Four Process Activities: RetailSync Case Study

| Stage | Specification | Development | Validation | Evolution |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kickoff | Weak, only a rough idea and had unwritten requirements | Present but started too early | Missing, nothing checked with warehouse staff | Missing no changes needed |
| Development | Weak, worked from assumptions and didnt include warehouse staff | Weak, little teamwork, no coding standards or reviews | Missing, no proper testing or user feedback | Missing |
| A Change of Plan | Weak, new requirement not documented | Weak, patched into the existing system instead of redesigning | Missing, not properly tested or checked with users | Weak, change was poorly planned |
| Testing | Weak, no proper requirements or test cases | Present, the devs fixed the crashes when testing | Weak, testing for only two days and no warehouse staff were involved | Missing |
| Go-Live | Missing, important requirements found too late | Present, fixes were needed after release | Weak, users tested it only after launch | Present |

The biggest failure is the weak specification at the beginning because it only gave a rough idea of what was needed and because of this it led to assumptions and problems with testing and the system failing when it went live.

## Researching a Software failure 
The Mars Climate Orbiter failed in 1999 while approaching Mars. The problem was that different parts of the project used different measurement units. One system used English units and another used metric which caused incorrect trajectory calculations and the the spacecraft ended up going much closer to Mars than planned, the error was not caught during checking and testing which meant it was discovered too late.

This links to complexity, large systems are made up of many different parts and teams so they need to work together correctly and a small mistake like this can cause the whole project to fail.
